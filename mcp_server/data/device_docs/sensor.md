# Sensor Troubleshooting (motion / water / contact sensors)

## Sensor shows "offline" or drops off intermittently
1. Check battery level first — sensors are typically battery-only, and connectivity drops are the most common symptom of a low battery.
2. Confirm the sensor is within range of the hub — sensors have shorter range than main hub-connected devices like thermostats.
3. Replace the battery if below 15%, then wait 2-3 minutes for it to reconnect.

## False or missed motion alerts
1. Check placement — sensors angled toward windows, HVAC vents, or areas with pets can cause false positives.
2. Sensitivity settings can be adjusted in the app if the customer reports either too many or too few alerts.

## Water sensor not detecting known moisture
1. Confirm the sensor's probes are actually in contact with the wet surface — sensors detect via direct contact, not humidity in the air.
2. Sensors do not detect moisture through a barrier (e.g. under a rug); relocate if the failure area is regularly covered.

## Escalate to a human agent if
- Battery was recently replaced and the sensor still won't reconnect after 3+ attempts.
- Customer reports a water sensor failed to alert during an actual leak/flood (escalate as a priority/safety concern).
